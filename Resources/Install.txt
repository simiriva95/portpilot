PortPilot is free and open source, but it is not signed with an Apple
Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the first time ("PortPilot is damaged"
or "Apple could not verify PortPilot").

1. Drag PortPilot to Applications.
2. Open Terminal and run:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PortPilot.app
   codesign --force --deep --sign - /Applications/PortPilot.app
   open /Applications/PortPilot.app

Without Terminal: open PortPilot once, then go to System Settings >
Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" next to the PortPilot message.

Repeat after every update. Full guide and source code:
https://github.com/simiriva95/portpilot#sign-it-yourself
